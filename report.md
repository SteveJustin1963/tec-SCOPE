Here is a clear side-by-side comparison of the two Lithofin impregnators based on the provided technical datasheets:

| Feature                              | Column A: Lithofin NanoTOP (Art.-No. 025)                          | Column B: Lithofin MN Stain-Stop (Art.-No. 166)                     |
|--------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Target use / Best for**            | Professional/high-performance use                                  | General household and professional use on marble & natural stone  |
| **Base**                             | Organic solvents + nano-particle polysiloxanes                     | Solvent-based (slightly higher flash point)                        |
| **Appearance after application**     | Generally no change; some honed/brushed dark stones (e.g. Nero Assoluto) become noticeably darker | Usually no change; some stones become slightly darker             |
| **How it works (coating)**           | Forms a very thin invisible film inside the capillaries (nano-technology) | Coats the capillaries with a thin invisible film                   |
| **Penetration depth**                | Deep penetration due to nano-particles                             | Standard deep penetration                                          |
| **Stain resistance duration** (how long oil can sit without staining) | Polished granite: ~ 72 h (up to 168 h on some granites)  
Limestone: ~ 48 h | Polished marble/limestone: 12–48 h  
Granite/gneiss: up to ~ 60 h                                      |
| **Why it is better against stains**  | Nano-particles give longer dwell time before oil penetrates (especially strong on dense polished granites) | Very good, but generally shorter dwell time than NanoTOP on granite |
| **Water repellency**                 | Very strong                                                       | Very strong (reduces water absorption by 90–95 %)                   |
| **Breathability (vapour permeability)** | Maintained (stone stays breathable)                               | Only slightly affected (80–90 % permeability remains)              |
| **Durability of full protection**    | Approx. 3 years indoors

Touch-up with MN Stain-Stop or STAINSTOP >W< if needed earlier    | Kitchen counters/vanity tops: touch-up once a year recommended

| **Food-safe once cured**             | Yes (tested to EU 1935/2004)                                      | Yes (tested to EU 1935/2004)                                       |
| **Flash point**                      | ~16 °C (more flammable)                                           | ~32 °C (safer)                                                     |

| **Full effectiveness after**         | 5 days                                                            | 5 days                                                             |
| **Shelf life (closed)**              | Up to 2 years                                                     | Up to 3 years                                                      |

- Choose **Lithofin NanoTOP** if you have granite and want the absolute longest possible time (up to 72–168 hours) before oil can penetrate
